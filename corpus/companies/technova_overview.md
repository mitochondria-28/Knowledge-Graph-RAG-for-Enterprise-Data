# TechNova Corporation — Company Overview

## About TechNova

TechNova Corporation is an enterprise software company founded in 2010 and headquartered in Austin, Texas. The company specializes in integrated business intelligence, data infrastructure, and machine learning platforms for large enterprises across financial services, healthcare, and logistics sectors.

TechNova's flagship product, NovaSuite, is a comprehensive enterprise resource planning and analytics platform used by more than 1,400 organizations worldwide. NovaSuite provides modules for financial reporting, supply chain visibility, customer analytics, and operational dashboards.

As of 2024, TechNova employs approximately 3,800 people across offices in Austin, London, Singapore, and Toronto.

## Leadership

Robert Klein serves as Chief Executive Officer of TechNova Corporation. Klein joined TechNova in 2014 as Chief Revenue Officer before being appointed CEO in 2018. Under his leadership, TechNova has completed two strategic acquisitions and expanded its annual recurring revenue from $120M to over $480M.

Dr. Elena Vasquez serves as Chief Technology Officer. Vasquez joined TechNova in 2016 from a research position at Carnegie Mellon University, where she led distributed systems research. She oversees all engineering, architecture, and platform strategy decisions at TechNova.

Marcus Thompson is Vice President of Engineering and reports directly to Dr. Vasquez. Thompson manages the Engineering Department, which includes the Platform Team, the Data Engineering Team, and the DevOps Team.

## Engineering Department

The Engineering Department is TechNova's largest division, comprising over 900 engineers. The department is organized into three primary teams:

- **Platform Team** — responsible for core infrastructure, database systems, and developer tooling. The Platform Team maintains StellarDB, TechNova's primary distributed database, as well as the internal GraphQL Gateway used by all product teams. The team is led by Aisha Patel.

- **Data Engineering Team** — responsible for data pipelines, ETL infrastructure, and the DataBridge platform. Led by Sandra Müller.

- **DevOps Team** — responsible for deployment automation, container orchestration using Kubernetes, and infrastructure reliability. Led by Kevin Park.

## Strategic Acquisitions

TechNova has pursued an acquisition strategy to accelerate its technology capabilities:

In February 2022, TechNova acquired Stellar Systems Inc. for approximately $340 million. Stellar Systems was a data infrastructure company based in Seattle that developed StellarDB, a high-performance distributed database system. Following the acquisition, StellarDB became TechNova's primary database platform, replacing the company's legacy relational database infrastructure.

In August 2023, TechNova completed the acquisition of Apex Analytics for approximately $220 million. Apex Analytics, based in San Francisco, had developed ApexML, a production-grade machine learning platform used by data science and engineering teams. ApexML is now central to TechNova's AI product roadmap.

## Technology Partnerships

TechNova maintains a formal technology partnership with CloudBridge Ltd, a cloud infrastructure provider. The partnership enables TechNova's CloudSync service to synchronize NovaSuite data across customer environments.

TechNova also maintains a strategic partnership with DataForge Inc, focused on joint development of data interoperability standards.

## Products

TechNova's primary products include:

- **NovaSuite** — the core enterprise platform
- **NovaSuite Analytics** — a self-service analytics module built on NovaSuite
- **CloudSync** — a data synchronization service developed in partnership with CloudBridge Ltd

## Data Science Department

The Data Science Department is led by James Okafor, who holds the title of Director of Data Science. The department includes the ML Team, which is responsible for integrating ApexML into TechNova's products and running machine learning workloads at scale.
