# TechNova and CloudBridge Ltd — Technology Partnership

## Partnership Overview

TechNova Corporation and CloudBridge Ltd entered into a formal technology partnership agreement in March 2021. The partnership centers on joint development and go-to-market activities related to cloud data synchronization for enterprise customers who operate hybrid on-premises and cloud environments.

## About CloudBridge Ltd

CloudBridge Ltd is a cloud infrastructure company headquartered in Dublin, Ireland. Founded in 2014, CloudBridge specializes in secure, low-latency data synchronization between enterprise on-premises systems and major public cloud providers including AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform.

CloudBridge developed **CloudSync**, a data synchronization service that provides:

- Real-time bidirectional data replication between enterprise systems and cloud storage
- Conflict resolution mechanisms for concurrent data writes
- End-to-end encryption with customer-managed keys
- Compliance audit logging for regulated industries

CloudSync is used by TechNova to enable NovaSuite customers to mirror their operational data to cloud-based data lakes and warehouses in near-real time.

## Technical Integration

The TechNova and CloudBridge technical integration connects CloudSync to TechNova's GraphQL Gateway, allowing NovaSuite customers to configure synchronization rules through the NovaSuite user interface without requiring direct configuration of CloudBridge's API.

TechNova's Platform Team developed and maintains the connector between CloudSync and the GraphQL Gateway. This connector translates NovaSuite's internal data events into the CloudSync API format.

## Commercial Arrangement

Under the partnership, TechNova resells CloudSync as a bundled offering with NovaSuite Enterprise tier subscriptions. CloudBridge receives a revenue share for each NovaSuite customer that activates CloudSync.

DataForge Inc, a data interoperability company, also participates in a three-way data standards working group with TechNova and CloudBridge, focused on defining common schemas for enterprise data exchange.

## Current Status

The partnership is active as of 2024. TechNova's CloudSync integration is used by approximately 340 NovaSuite customers. TechNova and CloudBridge are jointly evaluating an expansion of the partnership to include real-time streaming data synchronization powered by Apache Kafka.
